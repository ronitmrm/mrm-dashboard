# Factory and app workflow — review draft

This describes what the app currently records, from customer demand to a Job Card ready for dispatch. It is a description of the software, **not yet a confirmed account of factory practice**. Each **UI** line names the visible route. Screens and actions depend on the signed-in person's access.

## Part lifecycle currently represented

1. **Customer demand.** Sales logs an Enquiry and its parts. Technical Review decides whether a part is feasible, an existing Product, or cannot be quoted. New parts go through Design; existing parts can reuse their approved Product. **UI:** Operational Entry → Data Entry → Commercial Entries → Enquiries → Log Enquiry → Send To Technical Review; Costing → Technical Review → Design Tasks.
2. **Part definition and price.** Design completes the Product details, BOM, files and controls. Product Parameter Costing sets the Product cost; Customer Parameter Costing sets the price for this customer and enquiry. Sales sends the complete quotation and follows it up. **UI:** Costing → Design Tasks → Start Task → Start Design → Design Complete → Product Parameter Costing → Customer Parameter Costing → Sales → Send Full Enquiry.
3. **Customer order.** The customer's Purchase Order is logged or imported. Each line is matched to a quoted part; price differences are decided. A Proforma Invoice is generated, sent and approved to release the order. **UI:** Operational Entry → Data Entry → Commercial Entries → Purchase Orders → open PO → Po Lines And Quote Decisions → Proforma Invoice → Approve Pi And Release Order.
4. **Production order and material.** A Work Order line is entered or imported for a production unit; its Job Card identifies the line. Raw Material inward is recorded against that Job Card. These are separate operational entries; the inspected commercial order flow does not itself create the Work Order. **UI:** Operational Entry → Data Entry → production unit → Work Order; then Raw Material Inward. Production unit → Job Cards → open Job Card.
5. **Plan and prepare.** The selected route, setup, cycle, tooling, machine, quality and calendar masters make the Job Card plannable. The planner can change priorities, routes and machines, and review the effect on other work. Store allocation of usable tools can constrain the plan. **UI:** Master Data → Data Entry → production unit → relevant master; production unit → Part Readiness / Planning Control / Planner Actions / Machine Detail.
6. **Run the part.** Shop Floor confirms material at the machine. Machinist setting, Quality approval and machine start follow; CNC omits Pre Setting. An operator's Production Session records output, downtime and rejection. Good output from an earlier setup is work in progress for the next route step. **UI:** production unit → Shop Floor Tasks → Machinist → Quality Control → Production Sessions → Start Session / Session Register.
7. **Inspect and finish.** First-piece and hourly checks are saved against production. Rejections can also be recorded at Checking, Assembly or Quality Control. Each planned setup is explicitly completed. The Job Card combines output, quality, downtime, material and delivery history. **UI:** production unit → First Piece Inspection / Quality Control / Job Cards → open Job Card; Quality Control → Rejection Entry.
8. **Ready for dispatch.** Only a Job Card whose planned setups are all Item Complete appears for Dispatch Approval. The selected approver records that approval. The app shows order and dispatch dates, but the inspected workflow ends at approval; it does not document picking, packing, shipment, invoice or carrier handover. **UI:** production unit → Job Cards → Job Card Actions → Dispatch Approval; Production Dashboard → Work Order Dispatch Overview.

At MRMPL, a directly purchased item is also a Part/Product. It follows the customer demand and pricing path but bypasses the raw-material and machine-production steps above. The app supports direct-purchase costing; the inspected Store purchase flow is separate from the customer PO flow.

## Customer, design and commercial work

- **Maintain customer and commercial reference data.** Staff create, correct, import, review and, where allowed, remove Customers, terms, material rates, process defaults and currency rates. These feed later enquiries and costing; issued documents keep their saved values. **UI:** Master Data → Data Entry / View Records → Universal → Customers or Commercial Pricing Masters.
- **Log and correct an enquiry.** Sales creates a draft with commercial terms and part lines, by form or import. Sales can update or delete unstarted lines, or delete the whole unstarted enquiry. Sending it to Technical Review starts the shared downstream queue; opening it does not. **UI:** Operational Entry → Data Entry → Commercial Entries → Enquiries → Log Enquiry → open enquiry → Add Line / Import Lines / Update Enquiry / Delete Enquiry / Send To Technical Review.
- **Review an enquiry part.** Technical Review records feasibility, an existing Product match, or a clarification need. Feasible parts reach Design; a commercial requote of an existing Product can go straight to Customer Costing. **UI:** Costing → Technical Review → open part → save review; Costing → Sales → Sales Clarification.
- **Design a new part or assembly.** Design starts a released task, checks the existing Portfolio, or fills Product Details, BOM, Files and Design Controls. Draft saves retain progress; Design Complete validates the complete definition and hands it to Product Costing. A Package or Assembly can contain linked existing Products and new components. **UI:** Costing → Design Tasks → Start Task → Select Product or Start Design → Product Details → BOM → Files → Design Controls → Design Complete.
- **Maintain the Product design and drawings.** The Product Portfolio shows the current read-only dossier and released history. Drawing History shows the latest drawing per part and past revisions. A released design or drawing changes through an Engineering Change Note, with Design review and approval; cost-impacting changes continue through costing. **UI:** Costing → Product Portfolio → open Product; Costing → Drawing History; Costing → Engineering Changes → Open An Engineering Change Note → Design.
- **Add an existing BOM component.** The Assembly / Bom Register also lets an authorized user link an existing component Product to an existing Package or Assembly parent with a quantity. **UI:** Costing → Assembly / Bom → Add Component → Add Bom Line.
- **Cost the Product.** Product Costing calculates the Product-owned base cost from material, process and BOM inputs. It can save a draft and complete a task; a completed task passes to Customer Costing. **UI:** Costing → Product Parameter Costing → open task → save draft / complete costing.
- **Cost the customer quote.** Customer Costing adds customer-specific scrap, profit, packaging, shipping and currency choices to the Product cost. Completing the price makes that enquiry part Ready for Sales; a draft stays editable. **UI:** Costing → Customer Parameter Costing → open task → save draft / complete costing.
- **Issue and follow up a quotation.** Sales reviews the whole enquiry, sends one quotation when every line is Ready or Cannot Quote, then records follow-up outcomes and next dates. Sent PDFs, prices and earlier versions remain available. **UI:** Costing → Sales → Sales Task List → Quote Details / Send Full Enquiry → Complete Follow-Up; Costing → Quote Register / Sales → Sent Quotes.
- **Revise a quotation.** Sales requests changes on the existing enquiry. Terms-only changes stay with Sales; price changes return to Customer Costing; technical changes use an ECN and may return through Product Costing. Issuing the new complete quotation closes the request while preserving old versions. **UI:** Operational Entry → View Records → Enquiries → open enquiry → Request Revision; Costing → Engineering Changes / Customer Parameter Costing → Sales → Send Full Enquiry.
- **Receive and decide a customer PO.** Staff log or import the PO and its lines, attach the original, match quoted parts and choose Keep Our Price or Accept PO Price when prices differ. Unmatched lines can create quote requests. A PO can be cancelled with a reason before approval. **UI:** Operational Entry → Data Entry → Commercial Entries → Purchase Orders → open PO → Add Po Line / Excel Line Import → Po Lines And Quote Decisions / Cancellation.
- **Issue and approve a PI.** Once PO lines and price decisions are resolved, staff generate a Proforma Invoice, mark it sent, then approve it to release the order. Sent PDF and spreadsheet are retained. **UI:** Operational Entry → Purchase Orders → open PO → Proforma Invoice → Generate Pi → Mark Pi Sent → Approve Pi And Release Order.
- **Revise prices in bulk.** Product revisions stage Product inputs and send affected customer prices for decisions; Customer revisions stage customer-specific changes. Both show status and read-only completed history. Price Revisions has another controlled staging and completion path; Pricing Input Update offers a previewed Excel route for applicable input changes. **UI:** Costing → Product Bulk Revision / Customer Bulk Revision → Send To Costing → relevant costing queue → complete; Costing → Price Revisions → Create Revision / Stage Selected Prices / Complete Revision; Costing → Pricing → Pricing Input Update.
- **Inspect pricing and website data.** Pricing and Price Revisions show the current Product base and Customer prices. Website Product Data enriches an existing Product for the public catalogue without changing its core Product classification. **UI:** Costing → Pricing / Price Revisions; Master Data → Website Products or Costing → Website Products → Open For Editing → Save Website Product.

## Store and material support

- **Maintain Store identities and prices.** Staff maintain classification, Item Types, locations, suppliers, vendors and supplier price revisions. An Item Type gets a permanent Asset Code; one quoted supplier price is active per supplier and item, with retained history and optional quote PDF. **UI:** Master Data → Data Entry / View Records → Universal → Store Masters → chosen master; Store → Stock → Asset Code → Supplier Quotes.
- **Request a stocked item.** A requester selects coded items, enters quantities and a Department, and submits one numbered request. It joins the allocation queue. **UI:** Store → Stock → Request Items → Continue with Selected Request Items → Request Details → Release Request to Store.
- **Request an item without a code.** A requester describes the missing item. Store later links the request to an existing or newly created Item Type; it cannot be allocated while unresolved. **UI:** Store → New Item Requests → Request a New Item; Store → New Item Request Register → Resolve.
- **Buy Store goods.** Store selects items and effective supplier quotes; the app creates one PO per supplier and retains the issued PDF. A request line can lead to the same Stock order form. **UI:** Store → Stock → Make Purchase Order → Save Supplier Purchase Orders; Store → Requests & Issues → Make Order; Store → Purchase Register.
- **Receive goods.** Store records received quantities against open PO lines, singly or in a selected bulk receipt. Supplier bill and warranty evidence belong to the receipt; non-consumable units get individual IDs. The same Purchase Register shows partial and complete receipt status. **UI:** Store → Purchase Register → Receive Items → Receive Into Main Store; or select lines → Receive Selected.
- **Allocate and issue stock.** Store issues each request line or a same-Department batch from current available stock. Consumables reduce quantity; non-consumables require available individual Unit IDs. Shortages leave the line open and may lead to Make Order. **UI:** Store → Requests & Issues → Request Allocation Queue → Save Allocation / Allocate Selected in Full.
- **Track a physical asset.** Staff open an Asset Code for identity, drawing and supplier quotes, then an individual Unit ID for holder, movement, returns, lifecycle status, maintenance and calibration. A paid repair can create a Repair PO; movement and work history remain with the same unit. **UI:** Store → Stock → Asset Code → Overview / Drawings / Supplier Quotes → Unit ID → Move / Assign Asset / Create Repair PO / Add Timetable / Complete Maintenance / Update Status.

## Production planning and execution

- **Maintain production masters.** Unit-specific route, setup name, cycle, tooling, machine, quality parameters, checklist, holidays and breaks support production. Shared parameters, instruments, maintenance and rejection lists are company-wide. Staff create, edit, import, export and review records; a referenced master needs a valid replacement before deletion. **UI:** Master Data → Data Entry / View Records → production unit or Universal → selected master.
- **Enter a Work Order.** A production unit saves or imports order lines; each Job Card remains its own line, even if PO and part match another line. A line can be cancelled with a reason if its session is closed and it is not already dispatched. **UI:** Operational Entry → Data Entry / View Records → production unit → Work Order; production unit → Job Cards → open Job Card.
- **Record RM inward and shortages.** Each inward entry adds kilograms to a Job Card rather than replacing earlier receipts. The first receipt starts the historical finish baseline; the Job Card shows received and required material. **UI:** Operational Entry → Data Entry / View Records → production unit → RM Inward; production unit → Job Cards → open Job Card → Overview / Delivery.
- **Check readiness and plan the route.** Part Readiness flags missing masters; Planning Control and Machine Detail show route, machine and capacity issues. The current finish estimate changes with remaining output and constraints, while the initial receipt-based planned finish stays historical. **UI:** production unit → Part Readiness → resolve master; Planning Control / Machine Detail / Job Cards.
- **Assess a proposed order.** Before Purchase confirms it, the planner uploads proposed lines, supplies RM-ready dates, calculates tentative dates, reviews feasible combinations and approves a proposal. Approval creates no PO or capacity reservation. **UI:** production unit → Planner Actions → New Action → Order Acceptance Planning → Calculate Tentative Dates → Approve Proposal and Finish.
- **Change the live plan.** The planner can change priority or route, move a setup, add a parallel machine, mark a machine unavailable, or reject RM. The app previews impact and retains decisions for review; unresolved or overdue machine issues return to a review queue. **UI:** production unit → Planner Actions → New Action → choose action → Review Impact → Confirm Decision; Pending Review / Active Machine Issues / Decision History.
- **Place RM and complete setting.** Shop Floor records RM at the machine. Machinist starts Pre Setting where applicable, completes the setup checklist and Setting; Quality approves; Machinist starts the machine. CNC begins with Setting instead of Pre Setting. **UI:** production unit → Shop Floor Tasks / Machinist / Quality Control → action on the machine; Shop Floor Status.
- **Run and close a production session.** Shop Floor selects the planned machine and operator, then starts a shift session. It records downtime and output; Quality records rejected pieces. Closing records weight or machine-counter output and a reason such as Shift Ends or Item Complete. A later shift starts a new session. **UI:** production unit → Production Sessions → Start Session → machine → Start; Session Register → open session → Start/Close downtime / Rejection / End.
- **Handle downtime and breakdown.** A coded downtime stays open until resolved or carried to the next shift. Bulk Breakdown starts downtime on the reviewed running sessions. A machine-linked maintenance breakdown remains open until repair, blocking another session on that machine. **UI:** production unit → Production Sessions → Start downtime / Bulk Breakdown / Unresolved downtime; Maintenance → Mechanical → Start Breakdown / Complete.
- **Correct production evidence.** Authorized staff may correct a closed session's end/output or add missed downtime or rejection with a reason; the correction history remains. Separate operational entry accepts Software Production Output where that form is used. **UI:** production unit → Production Sessions → Session Register → Correct / View Session; Operational Entry → Data Entry → production unit → Software Production Output.
- **Inspect the first part and hourly output.** Quality uses the current unit's inspection parameters, saves first-piece reports and hourly checks, and can correct saved readings with a reason. Saved checks keep the definitions used at the time. **UI:** production unit → First Piece Inspection → report / reports; production unit → Quality Control → Hourly Quality Check → Register / Report.
- **Record further rejections.** Quality records Checking, Assembly or Quality Control rejection against a Job Card. The Rejection Register combines this with setup and in-process rejection events without re-entering those events. **UI:** Quality Control → Rejection Entry → Find Job Card → Rejection entry; ISO Document → Rejection Register.
- **Complete setups and approve dispatch.** Shop Floor closes the final session for a setup as Item Complete. Once every planned setup is complete, an eligible Planner or Shop Floor employee approves the Job Card for dispatch. The Job Card remains the place to review masters, material, setup, output, quality, downtime, delivery and full log. **UI:** production unit → Production Sessions → Session Register → End → Item Complete; production unit → Job Cards → Job Card Actions → Dispatch Approval → open Job Card.

## People, facilities, documents and administration

- **Maintain posts and employees.** HR maintains Departments, Designations, Job Templates, Approved Posts, Combined Approved Posts and Employee Master assignments. Vacant approved posts can create recruitment jobs; employment events and letters stay with the person, even after a replacement. **UI:** Master Data → Data Entry / View Records → Universal → HR; HR & Recruitment → Employee Master / Job Posts.
- **Recruit a candidate.** HR logs a Candidate, assigns one or more candidates to an open Job, tracks conversations, schedules and records interview rounds, then records acceptance or withdrawal. An accepted candidate can receive an Offer Letter and reserve an Approved Post; HR explicitly confirms joining or records Did Not Join. **UI:** Master Data → Data Entry → Universal → HR → Candidates → Log Candidate; HR & Recruitment → Search Candidate → assign to Job Posts → Interview Schedule / Interview Workspace → open Job → appointment action; Employee Master.
- **Correct or close recruitment records.** HR can update candidate details, correct the latest interview assessment or a pending appointment with a reason, close/reopen a Job as allowed, record resignation and replacement, and issue later employment letters at their lifecycle points. History remains available. **UI:** Master Data → View Records → Universal → HR → Candidates → open Candidate; HR & Recruitment → Job Posts → open Job / Interview Workspace / Employee Master.
- **Report and resolve maintenance requests.** An employee submits one problem; a manager approves, rejects or returns it and confirms category and priority. Electrical, Plumbing or Mechanical staff move approved work through In Progress and Completed; the manager closes it. Mechanical also includes scheduled machine tasks and breakdowns. **UI:** Maintenance → All Requests → New Maintenance Request; Manager Approval → decision; Electrical / Plumbing / Mechanical → work list → Complete; Manager Approval → Close.
- **Create and release controlled documents.** Authors save SOPs, policies, work instructions, notices or uploaded controlled documents as drafts. Quality submits them, the responsible manager approves or rejects, and Quality releases an immutable revision. A later change creates a new draft revision; readers see released documents in the Master Document List and published registers where offered. **UI:** Document Templates → document type → Data Entry / View Records → open document → Submit for Approval / Approve / Final Release; ISO Document → Master Document List / relevant published register.
- **Maintain the document index and monitoring.** Quality keeps control metadata, review and record obligations, locations and revision history. A responsible person can confirm due monitoring without copying an external record into the app. **UI:** ISO Document → Master Document List → open document → Control Details / Revisions / Monitoring / Transactions.
- **Read operational ISO registers.** The measuring-instrument list comes from Store Item Types; machine maintenance plan and register come from machine maintenance work. They link back to the owning records. **UI:** ISO Document → Measuring Instrument Register / Machine Maintenance Plan / Machine Maintenance Register.
- **Manage access and retained files.** Administrators create roles, staff login accounts and page/task grants, and can inspect or explicitly delete retained files with a reason. Staff can change their own password. **UI:** Access Administration → Access Administration → Staff Access / Application Roles; Access Administration → Artifacts; Password & Security.
- **Use a personal dashboard.** A user sees permitted work and metrics, adds supported analytics widgets, and arranges or removes their own widgets. This does not change factory records. **UI:** Dashboard → My Dashboard / My Analytics / My Workspace → Add to My Dashboard.

## Domain model — in progress

These are the factory meanings supplied in the interview. The workflows above describe the app's current coverage.

### Demand and identity

- **Part:** An item MRMPL supplies to a Customer, whether MRMPL manufactures it or buys it directly.
- **Product:** Another name for a Part at MRMPL.
- **Customer:** A company that buys Products from MRMPL.
- **Enquiry:** A customer's document or request asking MRMPL to quote for Parts.
- **Customer Purchase Order:** A customer's order for Parts that MRMPL must make or buy and dispatch to that Customer.
- **Work Order:** An order from Purchase to one internal production, surface-finishing or checking department to carry out work. Each department receives and enters its own Work Order.
- **Job Card:** A unique code assigned to each department's Work Order so its production and rejection can be tracked.

### Factory resources and people

- **Production Unit:** A department where Parts are produced.
- **Raw Material:** The rods used to manufacture Parts in production departments.
- **Store Item Type:** The classification of an item kept in Store. It receives a unique number used to track the item, its documents and its movements.
- **Machine:** Equipment in a Production Unit used either to manufacture Parts directly or to support production.
- **Employee Assignment:** An Employee's placement in a particular Approved Job Post.
- **Controlled Document:** An organizational document created for certification or another activity carried out at MRMPL.

### Factory meaning versus current app coverage

- **Part** and **Product** are synonyms, including directly purchased items. The app's direct-purchase Product is consistent with this meaning.
- Factory **Work Orders** and **Job Cards** are separate for each department, including surface finishing and checking. The app's current Work Order entry is available only for its four named production units; no corresponding surface-finishing or checking Work Order entry was found in this pass.
